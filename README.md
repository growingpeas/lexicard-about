# Wordlamp

**Tap a word in your reading app and its definition opens on top, offline.**

Look up any word without leaving your book. Select a word in your reading app, choose Wordlamp, and its definition opens in a small window on top of the page. Everything works offline, from dictionaries kept on your phone.

If ColorDict no longer downloads its dictionaries, Wordlamp answers the same call from Moon+ Reader and other reading apps: choose it as the external dictionary and keep reading.

## What it does

- Opens on top of your reading app, at the size and place the app asks for, then gets out of the way
- Reads the dictionary files you already own (StarDict format: .ifo, .idx, .dict, compressed or not)
- Downloads free dictionaries in the app: more than 300 language pairs, checked and unpacked on the phone
- Answers the text selection menu and text shared from any app, not only reading apps
- Shows formatted entries, with links from one word to another
- Search the dictionaries yourself from the app at any time

## Privacy

No account and no sign-in. Your dictionaries and your lookups stay on your phone. The app goes online only when you download a free dictionary.

Works with reading apps that call an external dictionary through the ColorDict intent, such as Moon+ Reader, and with any app that offers the text selection menu. Free dictionaries come from FreeDict, each under its own licence.
Not affiliated with ColorDict, Moon+ Reader, StarDict or FreeDict.
